Privacy Policy
Last updated: May 1, 2026
This Privacy Policy describes how Hon Ming Yip ("I", "me", or "my") handles information in the DayTrader mobile application ("the App").
Information I Collect
The App does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data to external servers. All data you enter remains stored locally on your device.
The following data is stored locally on your device only:

Trade records you manually enter (date, ticker, P&L, notes)
Screenshots you attach to trade entries
App settings and preferences

Photos and Camera
The App requests access to your Photo Library solely to allow you to attach trade chart screenshots to your journal entries. Photos are stored locally on your device and are never transmitted to any server.
Notifications
The App requests permission to send notifications solely to remind you about upcoming economic calendar events that you have opted into. No notification data is shared with third parties.
Third-Party Services
The App fetches economic calendar data from a public third-party API (ForexFactory). No personal information is sent to this service.
Data Storage
All your trading data is stored locally on your device using iOS standard storage. You may export and delete your data at any time through the App's Settings.
Children's Privacy
The App is not directed at children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.
Changes to This Policy
I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
